Trust & Protection

How We Protect Your Money

Your security and trust are the foundation of everything we do. Learn how Ijevo safeguards your funds, prevents fraud, and complies with strict financial regulations.

Safeguarded Client Funds

In accordance with UK regulations, all customer money is held in segregated safeguarding accounts with regulated tier-1 UK clearing banks (such as Barclays and NatWest).

Your funds are completely separated from Ijevo's own operational funds. We never invest, lend, or risk customer deposits under any circumstances.

Strict Regulatory Supervision

Ijevo operates in strict compliance with the UK Payment Services Regulations and is registered and supervised by HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) as a Money Service Business (MSB) for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) controls.

We strictly follow international Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) and international sanction screening standards.

Bank-Grade Data Protection

All your sensitive information, payment tokens, and identity records are shielded using state-of-the-art encryption protocols both at rest and in transit.

We adhere to European GDPR privacy standards. Your data is never sold, shared, or rented to third-party advertisers.

Real-Time Fraud Prevention

Our platform monitors transactions 24/7 with automated intelligence to detect unauthorized account access, suspicious patterns, or card theft.

Every card payment requires 3D Secure 2.0 (Verified by Visa / Mastercard Identity Check) and biometric Face ID or fingerprint confirmation.

Safety Tips

Keeping Your Account Secure

Simple habits to keep your money and account protected from scammers.

Never Share Your Passcode

Ijevo staff will never call, message, or email you asking for your one-time SMS verification code or password.

Verify Recipient Details

Always double check your recipient's name and account number before confirming your transfer.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you ever suspect unauthorized access to your account, freeze your account in the app or contact support immediately.
