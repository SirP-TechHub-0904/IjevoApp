How Sending Money with Ijevo Works

Sending money back home takes less than 3 minutes. Here is the complete step-by-step guide for both you and your recipient.

For The Sender

Sending Money in 4 Quick Steps

1

Sign Up & Verify ID

Download our app or use the website. Registration takes 60 seconds. A quick snapshot of your UK driver’s license or passport verifies your identity securely.

2

Choose Country & Amount

Enter how much you want to send. We immediately display our guaranteed exchange rate, transfer fee (£0.00 on qualifying corridors), and exact payout total.

3

Add Recipient Details

Choose their bank (Access, GTBank, Zenith, Kuda, etc.) or mobile wallet (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo). Enter their account number. We save them for 1-tap re-sends.

4

Pay & Track Live

Pay via UK Open Banking (Monzo, Barclays, Lloyds, Revolut) or Debit Card. The money is disbursed immediately and arrives in under 60 seconds!

For The Recipient

Effortless & Instant for Loved Ones

No App Download Needed

Your family members and loved ones do NOT need to download the Ijevo app or create an account to receive money.

Direct Credit & Instant SMS

Funds land directly in their existing local bank account or mobile wallet. Their bank sends them an immediate credit alert SMS.

Zero Recipient Deductions

Your recipient receives 100% of the funds sent. There are never any deductions, cash-out penalties, or unexpected fees.
