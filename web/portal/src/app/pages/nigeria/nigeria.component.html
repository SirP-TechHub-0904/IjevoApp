Frequently Asked Questions About Sending to Nigeria

Does my recipient need to have an Ijevo account?

No. Your recipient does not need an Ijevo account. The funds are deposited directly into their regular Nigerian bank account (Access, GTB, Zenith, etc.) or digital wallet (Kuda, OPay, PalmPay).

Are there any hidden fees or deduction charges at the recipient end?

Zero hidden fees. Your recipient receives the exact Naira amount displayed on your confirmation screen. There are no surprise deduction charges.

Is sending money to Nigeria with Ijevo compliant with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) rules?

Yes. All remittances are processed in full adherence to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) International Money Transfer Operator (IMTO) regulations and UK HMRC Money Service Business guidelines.
