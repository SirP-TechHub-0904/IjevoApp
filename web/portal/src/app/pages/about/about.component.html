Why We Started Ijevo

Built by the Diaspora, for the Diaspora

Every year, millions of hard-working people across the UK and Europe send billions of pounds back home to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and emerging economies to support loved ones, cover tuition, build homes, and invest in small family businesses.

Yet traditional high-street banks and legacy remittance shops continue to charge exorbitant fees, hide abusive FX margins inside artificial exchange rates, and force families to wait 3 to 5 business days just to receive emergency funds.

We founded Ijevo to dismantle these outdated barriers. By directly connecting UK and European real-time banking rails to domestic African interbank networks and mobile money switches, we deliver funds in seconds at transparent, fair market rates.

Our Core Values

  • Radical Transparency: Guaranteed rates disclosed before you send. No surprise deduction fees.
  • Uncompromising Speed: Every transfer settles in seconds, not days.
  • Family First: We treat every pound transferred with the care and urgency it deserves.
  • Bank-Grade Safety: Regulated, supervised, and safeguarded with segregated client accounts.
