Data Protection & GDPR

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026. How Ijevo collects, protects, and respects your personal and financial information.

1. Who We Are

Ijevo Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller responsible for your personal information collected through our website, mobile application, and payment services in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Identity Information: Full name, date of birth, residential address, government ID details (passport, driver's license), and selfie biometrics for anti-fraud identity verification.
  • Contact Information: Email address, mobile telephone number.
  • Financial Data: Bank account details, payment card information (tokenized), transaction history, and recipient bank account / mobile money details.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and operating system used to access our services.

3. Why We Process Your Data (Lawful Basis)

We process your personal information on the following legal grounds:

  • To perform our contract with you: To execute payment orders and remit funds to your chosen beneficiaries.
  • To comply with legal obligations: To perform statutory Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF), and international sanctions screening required by UK law.
  • Legitimate interests: To prevent fraud, secure our infrastructure, and improve customer service.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical and organizational safeguards. All customer identity records and payment data are encrypted at rest using strong AES-256 standard and transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 encryption. Access is strictly limited to authorized personnel with multi-factor authentication.

5. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under UK data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to statutory retention periods required by financial regulators (e.g., 5-year AML record retention rules).
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
