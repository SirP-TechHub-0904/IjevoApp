How to Send Money

Sending money with Ijevo is designed to be as fast as sending a text message:

  1. Download & Register: Download our app for iOS or Android and complete 60-second sign-up.
  2. Select Amount: Pick your destination country and enter the amount you wish to transfer.
  3. Enter Recipient: Choose their local bank or mobile wallet (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, etc.) and enter their account number.
  4. Pay Securely: Authorize payment using your debit card or UK bank app (Monzo, Barclays, Lloyds, Revolut).

How to Receive Money

We make receiving money 100% effortless for your family and loved ones:

  • No App Required: Recipients do not need to download the Ijevo app or register an account.
  • Direct Bank Credit: Funds land straight in their regular bank account within 60 seconds.
  • Mobile Money Support: Direct credit to M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and digital wallets.
  • Zero Deductions: Recipients get 100% of the funds with zero cash-out penalties or hidden charges.
